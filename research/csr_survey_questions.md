# CSR Survey — Stage 1 of CSR research

**Owner:** Aud
**Last updated:** 2026-05-13
**Status:** Draft — ready for Rosie review before sending

---

## Two-stage research approach

Per Aud's 2026-05-13 plan: CSR research happens in two stages.

| Stage | Format | Audience size | Time per respondent | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Survey (this doc)** | Anonymous web form (Google Forms / Typeform) | 30–60 responses | 5–7 minutes | Broad feedback on concept, pricing, features. Quantitative + short-answer. |
| **2 — Prototype testing** | 1:1 video call walkthrough — see [user_testing_csr_guide.md](user_testing_csr_guide.md) | 5 selected from survey respondents | 60–75 minutes | Deep qualitative reactions to the actual product flow. |

The survey opt-in invites respondents to volunteer for follow-up prototype testing. Stage 2 participants are selected from that pool to match a mix of work types, experience levels, and geographies.

---

## Survey design notes

- **Keep it under 7 minutes.** CSRs are busy professionals; longer surveys collapse the response rate.
- **Mostly multiple choice and short numeric.** One or two free-text fields for richer feedback.
- **Anonymous by default**, optional contact field for follow-up.
- **No pricing-anchoring at the top.** Lead with their current reality, then introduce DS concepts. Avoids biasing answers.
- **Built in Google Forms or Typeform.** Both support conditional logic if needed.
- **Distribution:** Rosie's network, CSR Facebook groups (Court Reporter Rates Discussion + CA-specific groups), Cal-CCRA outreach. Target 30–60 responses over 2 weeks.

### Section 3 — Concept reaction (6 questions)

**Concept paragraph shown to respondents (~75 words):**

> Dewey Sholes is a new platform that connects California attorneys directly with court reporters for hearings and audio transcription. Reporters keep 100% of their stated rate. Attorneys pay the reporter's rate plus a 12% platform fee. There's no agency in between. Reporters set their own rate within a 20% band of platform defaults. The platform handles scheduling, billing, transcript delivery — replacing the admin work agencies typically charge for.

**Q11.** Based on that description alone, how interested would you be in trying this platform?

## What we're trying to learn from each section

| Section | What success looks like | What failure looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Mix of years, work types, counties (so we know who's responding) | All respondents look the same — survey isn't reaching the whole CSR community |
| Current economics | Real data on agency splits + direct rates — fills the [pricing_questions_rosie_and_csrs.md](pricing_questions_rosie_and_csrs.md) Q6/Q7 gap that Rosie couldn't answer | Most respondents say "I don't know" — we still need user testing to learn |
| Concept reaction | Top concerns are addressable (volume, trust, payment) and excitement is real (no agency cut, direct relationships) | Top concerns are existential (concept itself rejected, "agencies are fine") |
| Featured reporter (Q14) | Meaningful interest (>40% say "yes") — validates Rosie's idea | Low interest (<20%) — defer post-pilot indefinitely |
| Pricing perception | 12% feels reasonable, $99 listing feels reasonable | Pricing rejected — needs rethinking before pilot |
| Pilot interest | >50% "very likely" at $0 listing — supply-side wedge is real | <30% "very likely" — supply acquisition harder than projected |

## Distribution plan

- **Soft launch:** Rosie sends to 10 trusted CSR contacts first to catch survey-design issues. 1 week to refine.
- **Wave 1:** Cal-CCRA mailing list, Rosie's professional network, CSR Facebook groups (Court Reporter Rates Discussion is 11K members — Caldarella's audience). 2 weeks.
- **Wave 2:** Boost via paid LinkedIn promotion targeting CA CSRs ($100 budget). 1 week.
- **Honorarium for completion:** $10 digital gift card to first 50 completers (caps cost at $500). Or skip if response rate is strong organically.

Target n = 30–60 completed responses. Subset of ~10 volunteers self-select for Stage 2 prototype testing.
