# CA legal industry events: June - December 2026

This document catalogs California legal industry events between June and December 2026 where Shorthanders can reach paralegals, legal assistants, and law firm support staff – the operational decision-makers for booking court reporters. The research targets venues where Rosie can attend personally (Bay Area, HIGH priority) or where a paid law-student ambassador can represent the brand (SoCal MEDIUM, Sacramento/other LOW).

Notes on data quality: most paralegal associations in California use Wild Apricot or StarChapter member-portals that gate event detail pages behind a login or against the WebFetch tool used here. As a result, several specific dates, sponsorship tiers, and table prices are marked "Unknown – contact organizer." Where prior-year pricing is documented publicly, that has been noted as a planning anchor. Cite-checking and price confirmation should be the next step for the highest-priority events.

A consistent pattern across CA paralegal associations: monthly luncheon MCLEs ($25-$50/seat for non-members) are cheap, low-commitment ways to send an ambassador with swag; annual fall conferences are where most vendor exhibitor spend happens (typical CA paralegal-conf exhibitor table: $500-$1,500 historically).

## Bay Area events (HIGH priority)

### SFPA monthly MCLE luncheons - Recurring (June - December 2026)
- **Host:** San Francisco Paralegal Association (SFPA)
- **Location:** San Francisco; usually downtown law-firm conference rooms or Zoom hybrid
- **Attendees:** SF paralegals, legal assistants, paralegal students; typically 20-60 per session
- **Costs:** Member free or $10-$25; non-member $25-$50 (historic). Sponsor/table: Unknown – contact organizer
- **Vendor pitching allowed:** Limited – typically a 2-3 minute "sponsor spotlight" if you sponsor the lunch
- **Fit for Shorthanders:** Strong. Highly concentrated audience of exactly the operational buyers we target. Low cost of entry per touch.
- **Recommended action:** Send Rosie or a paid law-student ambassador to 2-3 sessions June-Dec; explore lunch sponsorship for one fall session ($300-$800 typical historic rate, confirm with SFPA).
- **Source:** [SFPA Calendar](https://www.sfpa.com/calendar) (calendar currently empty for the period; email info@sfpa.com for the publishing schedule)

### BASF Paralegal Section networking mixers & holiday party - Recurring through 2026
- **Host:** Bar Association of San Francisco (BASF), Paralegal Section
- **Location:** BASF offices, 201 Mission St., San Francisco, or partner venues
- **Attendees:** Paralegals, legal assistants, legal nurse consultants, attorney section liaisons; ~30-100 per event
- **Costs:** Mixers historically $15-$35 for non-members. Sponsor/table: Unknown – contact cle@sfbar.org
- **Vendor pitching allowed:** Mixers are social – no formal pitching, but business cards/swag table common with sponsorship
- **Fit for Shorthanders:** Very strong. BASF Paralegal Section is the Bay Area's most concentrated formal community of legal support staff. Holiday party (Dec) historically the largest single event of the year for this audience.
- **Recommended action:** Sponsor or co-sponsor the BASF Paralegal Holiday Party (December 2026) – single highest-value Bay Area touch point. Rosie attends in person.
- **Source:** [BASF Paralegal Section](https://www.sfbar.org/basf-sections/paralegal/), [BASF Events Calendar](http://www.sfbar.org/CALENDAR/list.aspx?type=&com=paralegal)
