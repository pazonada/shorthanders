# Swag & branded merchandise strategy

**Owner:** Aud
**Last updated:** 2026-05-02
**Status:** Draft — informed by Rosie's insights on what legal assistants want

---

## Strategic principle

Swag serves two competing goals — and the right item depends on which one you're optimizing for:

| Goal | What works | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Recipient delight** (they remember us) | Desk accessories, $10 gift cards, useful tools | Makes us feel personal and thoughtful. Rosie: "small dollar amounts but they definitely remember who got it for them." |
| **Brand impressions** (free advertising) | Items they carry in public — water bottles, totes, jackets | Every time they're seen using it, that's a brand impression. Compounds over months/years. |

The best items do both. The worst items (cheap pens, generic tchotchkes) do neither.

## Tiered swag plan

### Tier 1: "Hand to anyone interested" (~$3-5 per kit)

For walking through an exhibit hall, drop-in conversations, conference attendees who walk past our table.

**Recommended kit:**
- Branded high-quality pen ($1-2) — paralegals use pens constantly; quality pen = positive association
- Branded sticky notepad ($1-2) — desk item Rosie specifically called out
- Vinyl sticker with logo ($0.50) — for laptops, water bottles
- Business card with QR code ($0.10) — links to landing page

**Per ambassador kit (50 units):** ~$200

### Tier 2: "Qualified lead" (~$10-25 per item)

For people who hand over their business card, sign up for a demo, or have a real conversation. Think more carefully about what we're giving them.

**Recommended options (mix and match):**
- **Branded staple puller or letter opener** ($8-15 each, custom engraved) — Rosie's specific recommendation; legal assistants reach for these daily
- **Branded ceramic mug for the desk** ($6-10) — visible to everyone who walks by their workstation
- **$10 Starbucks gift card with branded card holder** ($12 total) — per Rosie: "they definitely remember who got it for them"
- **Quality canvas tote bag with subtle logo** ($8-15) — legal assistants carry stuff between courthouse, office, and home; tote = brand impressions in public

**Recommendation: lead with the $10 Starbucks gift card paired with a small branded item.** The gift card creates the personal moment Rosie described; the branded item gives us ongoing visibility.

### Tier 3: "We want to remember you" (~$30-50 per item)

For genuinely high-value contacts — paralegals at top-target firms, potential ambassadors, BASF section leadership, anyone who could be a multi-firm referrer.

**Recommended:**
- **Stanley-style insulated tumbler** ($30-40) — same item we use as the Tier 2 reward in our incentive program, so we're consistent. High-impressions item: it goes everywhere with them. This should be our "halo" gift.

**Limited quantity** — maybe 25-50 across all events for the year. Hand-selected recipients only.

### What we DON'T give out (despite the temptation)

- **T-shirts** — paralegals don't wear branded t-shirts to work; they live in dressers and become pajamas
- **Cheap pens** — get lost or thrown away within days; bad brand association
- **Stress balls / fidget toys** — ends up in landfill
- **Branded keychains** — nobody wants more keychains
- **Branded USB drives** — security-cautious legal industry won't plug random USBs into work computers
- **Branded mints / candy** — gone in days, no brand impression
- **Branded notebooks bigger than pocket size** — most paralegals use digital notes or firm-issued notebooks

## Apparel for ambassadors (their uniform)

**The Adidas track jacket idea is creative but probably wrong for this audience.** Track jackets read as "tech bro" or "marathon training" — they don't fit the conservative legal industry aesthetic. Ambassadors will look out of place at a paralegal CE seminar.

**Better choice: branded quarter-zip fleece or polo.**

| Option | Cost per unit | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branded Adidas track jacket with sewn patch | $90-120 | Standout, distinctive, "cool" | Too casual for legal events, ~2x cost, less professional |
| **Branded quarter-zip fleece (Eddie Bauer / Patagonia / Custom Ink)** | **$45-60** | **Professional, wearable in office, fits legal industry** | **Less distinctive than track jacket** |
| Branded polo shirt | $25-35 | Cheaper, summer-friendly | Too casual for some events, less weather-flexible |
| Branded button-down (oxford) | $35-50 | Most professional | Hard to get right size; ironing required |

**My recommendation: quarter-zip fleece for ambassadors as their primary uniform**, with a polo for warmer events. Both come from the same supplier (Custom Ink, 4imprint, or similar) for ~$70-90 per ambassador total.

**For 4 ambassadors at startup:** ~$320 in apparel + $80 in lanyards/badges = **~$400 upfront for the entire ambassador uniform program.**

### When to actually use the track jacket idea

The patches-on-track-jackets idea could work as a **special program** for **highly engaged Tier 2 referrers** (legal assistants who refer 5+ other legal assistants who sign up). It's a quirky, memorable thing — "we sewed our patch on a vintage track jacket and mailed it to you." That's the kind of personal touch people post about on social media.

But it's a **retention/referral reward**, not a giveaway or ambassador uniform. Limited quantity, hand-picked recipients, very personal.

## Initial swag investment

For the entire 6-month event program:

| Category | Quantity | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 kits (pens + notepads + stickers + cards) | 200 units (covers ~10 events) | $700 |
| Tier 2 items (mix: staple pullers + Starbucks gift cards + tote bags) | 60 total mix | $600 |
| Tier 3 items (Stanley-style tumblers, halo gift) | 25 units | $750 |
| Ambassador apparel (4 ambassadors: quarter-zip + polo + lanyard) | 4 sets | $400 |
| Shipping / fulfillment | n/a | $150 |
| **Total upfront** | | **~$2,600** |

That's higher than the $500-1,500 Phase 2 budget I'd previously suggested. **We should cut Tier 3 quantity to start.** Try 10 Stanley tumblers instead of 25 — saves $450, brings total to ~$2,150. Still over budget but defensible if events truly are our #1 acquisition channel.

**Alternative leaner version (~$1,500):**
- 100 Tier 1 kits ($350)
- 30 Tier 2 items, mostly $10 Starbucks gift cards + branded mugs ($350)
- 10 Stanley tumblers (Tier 3 halo) ($300)
- 4 ambassador apparel sets ($400)
- Shipping ($100)

This is achievable within the originally-stated Phase 2 budget. Reorder if/when revenue starts.

## What Rosie said (preserved verbatim)

> "Legal assistants want Stanley cups and desk accessories. Letter openers, staplers, staple pullers, paper clip organizers. Also gift cards. Small dollar amounts but they definitely remember who got it for them. Starbucks or a lunch spot. Maybe $10."

This is the most important input we have on what works for this audience. The plan above is built around it.
