## Part 2: Reporter FAQ

### What is Briscoe Sholes?

Briscoe Sholes is a platform where law firms order court transcripts and licensed court reporters fulfill those orders. Think of it as a single place where firms can find you, order from you, and pay you — without the back-and-forth emails, invoices, and check-chasing that usually comes with freelance transcript work.

### Who built this?

One co-founder is a California litigator with 22 years of practice who got tired of the transcript ordering process being so painful. The other has a technology background. The platform is built by people who understand how the court system actually works.

### How do I get work through the platform?

When a firm places a transcript order, the platform notifies reporters who match the job's requirements (geographic area, practice type, turnaround time, and availability). You see the job details — including compensation and deadline — and decide whether to accept. If you accept, the job is yours. If you don't, it goes to the next available reporter.

### How much do I get paid?

You receive the statutory per-word rate set by Government Code section 69950. The platform enforces these rates. They cannot be discounted by either party. Your expedite fees are set by you in your profile, and they're disclosed to the firm before they order.

### Who pays the platform fee?

The firm pays the platform fee. It's a percentage on top of the statutory rate. You receive the full statutory rate for your work.

### How fast do I get paid?

Within 48 hours of delivering the completed transcript. No invoicing. No collections. No 60-day waits. Payment is automatic via direct deposit.

### Do I have to accept every job?

No. You choose which jobs to accept based on your availability, geographic coverage, and preferences. There is no minimum volume requirement and no penalty for declining a job.

### Can I still work through agencies and take direct clients?

Yes. There is no exclusivity. The platform is one channel for getting work, not the only channel. You can work through agencies, take direct clients, and use Briscoe Sholes simultaneously.

### Am I an employee?

No. You are an independent contractor. AB 3136 (2019) specifically exempts licensed court stenographers from the ABC test under AB 5. The engagement satisfies the Borello test: you choose your own jobs, set your own schedule, use your own equipment, and can work for others simultaneously. You receive a 1099, not a W-2.

### What about my W-9?

You provide your W-9 once at signup. The platform handles 1099 reporting for all orders placed through it. You never have to send a W-9 to an individual firm again for platform orders.

### What types of work are available?

Two types, depending on the county and case. Live attendance work, where you attend a hearing and produce the transcript afterward. And audio transcription work, where a county uses electronic recording and you transcribe from the audio file. Your profile indicates which types you're available for.

### Do I need any special technology?

You need your existing CAT software and the ability to upload a completed transcript file. The platform handles everything else. There's no proprietary software to install.

### What about quality review? Does anyone edit my transcript?

No. Your certified transcript is your professional work product. The platform does not edit, alter, or review your transcripts. It's a logistics and payment layer, not a content layer.

### What if I have a problem with a firm or an order?

The platform includes a dispute resolution process for issues like scope changes, delivery disagreements, or cancellations. Details are provided during onboarding. The goal is to resolve issues quickly without either party chasing the other.

### How do I sign up?

Visit briscoesholes.com and register as a reporter. You'll need your active CSR license number, contact information, geographic coverage, availability preferences, W-9, and direct deposit details. We verify your license against the CRB database before activating your account.

### I have more questions. Who do I contact?

Email hello@briscoesholes.com or visit briscoesholes.com. We're happy to set up a quick call to walk you through anything.
