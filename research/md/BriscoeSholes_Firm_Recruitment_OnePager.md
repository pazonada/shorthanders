# Your firm won the hearing. Now you need the transcript by Monday.

Your client just spent a lot of money on a contested hearing. You won, but opposing counsel won't accept the proposed order, so now you need a certified transcript. You request it the next day.

Three weeks later, it shows up just under the wire. The reporter only turned it around that fast because he knows your firm personally, and he did the work before your paper check arrived. His only payment method is paper check. He still hasn't sent his W-9, so your partner has to nudge him for it. Again.

When you read the transcript, the record is clean. Your stumbles, false starts, interruptions from the bench, all smoothed out. You sound better on paper than you felt in the room. But that's because this particular reporter takes pride in his work. Not all of them do.

This is what "working" looks like in California courts today. It runs on personal relationships, favors, and professional goodwill. None of it is designed to work. It just sometimes does.

Now picture a reporter who doesn't know your firm. Who doesn't pick up. Who doesn't clean up the record. Who never sends the W-9.

## What we're not

- We're not a court reporting agency. We're a marketplace. Reporters are independent, licensed professionals who use their own tools and accept jobs on their terms.
- We don't set transcript rates. The per-word rate is fixed by California statute (Gov. Code § 69950). What we do is standardize expedite fees and show you the full cost before you order, so there are no surprises.
- We don't replace your favorite reporter. You can designate preferred reporters who get first right of refusal on your jobs.
