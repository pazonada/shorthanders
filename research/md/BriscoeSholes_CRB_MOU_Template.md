# Memorandum of Understanding
## Between the Court Reporters Board of California and Briscoe Sholes [Entity Name TBD]

*DRAFT TEMPLATE — March 13, 2026*
*This is a framework document. Legal counsel for both parties must review and revise before execution.*

---

## 1. Parties

**The Board:** Court Reporters Board of California, a regulatory body within the Department of Consumer Affairs, State of California.

**The Company:** Briscoe Sholes [legal entity name and type to be inserted after formation], a technology platform that connects California law firms with licensed certified shorthand reporters for court transcript ordering, payment, and delivery.

---

## 2. Purpose

This MOU establishes a framework for cooperation between the Board and the Company in areas of mutual interest, specifically: verification of CSR licensee status, coordination on Transcript Reimbursement Fund procedures, and information sharing related to the court reporter workforce and transcript access in California.

This MOU does not constitute an endorsement of the Company by the Board, nor does it create any obligation for the Board to promote, recommend, or direct licensees to the Company's platform.

---

## 3. Background

The Board is responsible for licensing and regulating certified shorthand reporters in California. The Board maintains a public database of licensee information and administers the Transcript Reimbursement Fund.

The Company operates a marketplace platform through which law firms can order court transcripts from licensed CSRs. The Company verifies that all reporters on its platform hold active CSR licenses. The Company supports TRF-eligible orders as part of its standard operations.

Both parties share an interest in maintaining professional standards for court reporting, increasing transcript access for California litigants, and supporting the licensed CSR workforce.

---

## 4. Scope of cooperation

### 4.1 License verification

**Current state.** The Company currently verifies CSR license status using the Board's public online license lookup tool at the time of reporter registration and periodically thereafter.

**Proposed cooperation.** The parties will explore whether a more efficient verification method is available or appropriate, which may include:

- Access to a bulk data extract or API of active licensee status (if technically feasible and permitted under applicable law)
- A formal verification request process where the Company submits license numbers and receives confirmation of current status
- An automated notification system where the Board alerts the Company if a licensee's status changes (suspension, revocation, expiration, or voluntary surrender)

The specific method, frequency, and technical implementation will be documented in an addendum to this MOU once agreed upon.

**Company obligations.** The Company will use licensee information solely for the purpose of verifying that reporters on its platform hold active licenses. The Company will not sell, share, or use licensee data for marketing, solicitation, or any purpose beyond platform eligibility verification. The Company will suspend a reporter's ability to accept new jobs through the platform within 24 hours of learning that their license is no longer active.

**Board obligations.** The Board will make reasonable efforts to respond to the Company's verification requests in a timely manner, using whatever method the parties agree upon. The Board is not responsible for errors in the Company's verification process or for consequences arising from a reporter's work on the platform.

### 4.2 Transcript Reimbursement Fund coordination

**Current state.** The Company's platform supports TRF-eligible orders as a workflow feature. The firm or SRL pays the reporter through the platform at the statutory rate, and the platform generates the TRF reimbursement application with all required fields pre-filled. The firm or SRL submits the application to the Board for reimbursement. The Company does not front the reimbursement or carry float on TRF orders.

**Proposed cooperation.** The parties will explore whether coordination can improve TRF processing efficiency, which may include:

- Confirmation that the Company's auto-generated TRF application paperwork meets the Board's current form and content requirements
- Notification to the Company of any changes to TRF application procedures, reimbursement timelines, or funding availability
- Aggregate data sharing from the Company on TRF-eligible order volume processed through the platform, which may assist the Board in assessing TRF utilization and resource allocation
- Exploration of whether the Board could accept electronically generated and submitted TRF applications (rather than requiring manual paper submission)

**Company obligations.** The Company will ensure that TRF applications generated through its platform contain accurate and complete information as required by the Board. The Company will not represent to users that TRF reimbursement is guaranteed. The Company will promptly update its TRF paperwork generation to reflect any procedural changes communicated by the Board.

**Board obligations.** The Board will make reasonable efforts to notify the Company of material changes to TRF procedures, application requirements, or funding availability.

### 4.3 Information sharing

**From the Company to the Board.** The Company is willing to share aggregate, anonymized data that may be useful for the Board's regulatory and workforce planning purposes, including:

- Number of licensed reporters active on the platform, by county
- Volume of transcript orders processed, by county and case type
- Average turnaround times, by order tier
- Reporter utilization rates (percentage of registered reporters accepting at least one job per quarter)

This data will be provided in aggregate form only. Individual reporter or firm data will not be shared without the consent of the relevant party unless required by law.

**From the Board to the Company.** The Company would benefit from advance notice of:

- Changes to licensing requirements or renewal procedures
- Changes to TRF funding levels, application procedures, or reimbursement timelines
- Proposed legislation or regulation affecting CSR licensing, court reporting practices, or transcript pricing
- Workforce data the Board publishes (e.g., active licensee counts, new applications, retirements) that may inform the Company's operations

The Board has no obligation to provide this information beyond what is publicly available, but the parties agree that proactive communication serves both parties' interests.

---

## 5. Data privacy and security

The Company will handle any licensee information received from the Board in accordance with applicable California law, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the Information Practices Act. The Company will implement reasonable security measures to protect licensee data, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and logging of data access.

If the Company experiences a data breach affecting licensee information received from the Board, the Company will notify the Board within 72 hours of discovery.

---

## 6. Intellectual property

Nothing in this MOU grants either party rights to the other party's intellectual property, trademarks, or proprietary systems. The Company will not use the Board's name, logo, or seal in marketing materials without prior written consent. The Company may state factually that it verifies CSR licenses through the Board's public database.

---

## 7. No endorsement

This MOU does not constitute an endorsement, recommendation, or approval of the Company, its platform, or its services by the Board. The Board makes no representations about the Company's business practices, financial stability, or suitability for any purpose. The Company will not represent or imply that the Board endorses the platform.

---

## 8. Term and termination

This MOU takes effect on the date of the last signature below and remains in effect for an initial term of one (1) year. The MOU renews automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party provides written notice of termination at least 60 days before the end of the current term.

Either party may terminate this MOU at any time with 30 days' written notice if the other party materially breaches any provision and fails to cure the breach within the notice period.

Upon termination, the Company will cease using any licensee data received directly from the Board (as distinct from publicly available data) within 30 days and will certify destruction in writing.

---

## 9. General provisions

**Amendment.** This MOU may be amended only by written agreement signed by both parties.

**Governing law.** This MOU is governed by the laws of the State of California.

**No agency.** Neither party is an agent, employee, or representative of the other. This MOU does not create a partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship.

**Entire agreement.** This MOU constitutes the entire understanding between the parties on the subjects covered. It supersedes any prior oral or written agreements on these subjects.

**Authority.** Each signatory represents that they have the authority to bind their respective organization to the terms of this MOU.
