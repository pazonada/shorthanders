## Where this lands relative to the bootstrapped reality

Per the existing `marketing_strategy_master.md` budget reality check, the platform is bootstrapped with no external funding. The Standard path (~$2,300) is at the upper edge of what's reasonable for a pilot marketing spend before any revenue. Recommendations:

- **Do the Lean path on Phase 1** (free email tools, manual research). The CSR side has the highest leverage and lowest marginal cost.
- **Do the Standard path on Phase 2** when triggered by hitting the 100-CSR floor. The postcard + chocolate combo is the differentiated channel; cheaping out on it defeats the purpose.
- **Defer the CSR referral gift-card spend** until referrals are actually happening.
- **Treat the comic illustration as a one-time investment** that pays off across the entire pilot. Spending $200 on a good illustrator is worth more than $50 on a forgettable one.
