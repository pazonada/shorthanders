## Pilot phase pricing (first 90 days)

### For CSRs
- **Listing fee waived** during pilot
- CSRs understand they are signing up to test the product while it's in beta
- Expected to respond to periodic surveys for product feedback
- After pilot: $99/year individual, $79/reporter/year firm tier

### For law firms
- **Platform fee reduced to 8%** (vs standard 12%) for firms that sign up during pilot
- The 8% rate extends through the **first full year** (not just the 90-day pilot)
- Firms understand the first 3 months is beta
- After Year 1: standard 12% rate applies

### Post-pilot pricing (standard)
- Law firms: 12% buyer-side transaction fee
- Individual CSRs: $99/year listing fee
- Firm tier: $79/reporter/year (3-10 reporters)

## Targeted recruitment: near-retirement CSRs

Per Rosie's suggestion: search the CRB database by license number to identify CSRs who have been licensed the longest (older license numbers = earlier in career). These reporters may be close to retirement and represent a unique recruitment opportunity — they have deep experience, established reputations, and may be interested in a platform that simplifies their remaining working years.

**Action item:** Build a filtered list from the CRB data sorted by original issue date (earliest first) and cross-reference with Bay Area geography for targeted outreach.
