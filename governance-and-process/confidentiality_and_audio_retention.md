## Confidentiality of audio files

### Public hearings
- Audio from public court hearings is publicly available
- No confidentiality restrictions apply
- CSRs are not bound by confidentiality obligations for public proceedings

### Private / privileged audio
- Responsibility for handling attorney-client privileged audio rests with the **law firm**, not the CSR or the platform
- The CSR is not implicated in confidentiality breaches — the firm controls what audio they share and how
- Shorthanders does not need an NDA at reporter onboarding for this reason

### Platform position
- Shorthanders transfers files; we do not review, edit, or retain audio beyond the retention period
- No contractual confidentiality language is required for CSRs regarding attorney-client privilege
- If a firm uploads privileged audio, that is the firm's decision and responsibility

## Audio file retention

### Retention period: 90 days after delivery acceptance

| Event | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Transcript delivered + attorney accepts | 90-day retention clock starts |
| Transcript delivered + auto-release (2 business days, no response) | 90-day retention clock starts |
| 90 days after delivery acceptance | Audio file permanently deleted from platform |
| Grievance filed within 90 days | Audio retained until grievance resolved + 30 additional days |

### Rationale
- After payment and transcript delivery, the platform has no ongoing need for the audio
- 90 days provides a window for the attorney to raise any issues
- Aligns with general commercial dispute timelines

### Future action item
- Research whether the Uniform Commercial Code (UCC) specifies a specific challenge window for delivered professional services
- If UCC specifies a longer period, adjust retention accordingly
- Until confirmed, 90 days is a reasonable default

## Transcript file retention

- Transcripts (the delivered .pdf/.txt files) are stored in the **attorney's account** as their copy
- Attorneys can download their transcripts at any time
- CSRs retain their own copies and original stenographic notes per their professional obligations
- The platform is **not** the custodian of the record — see [Transcript retention & custody policy](transcript_retention_and_custody_policy.md) for full details on regulatory boundaries
- Transcripts are **never** resold, redistributed, or made available to any party other than the original ordering attorney
