# Late delivery & deplatforming policy

**Owner:** Aud & Rosie
**Last updated:** 2026-03-26
**Status:** Draft — confirmed in Rosie call 3/26

---

## Delivery deadlines

- **Attorney-specified deadline:** If the attorney sets a delivery date in the order, that date is the deadline.
- **No deadline specified:** Default deadline is **45 calendar days** from job acceptance.
- **Grace period:** 24 hours after the stated deadline before the delivery is counted as "late."
- **Transparency:** At booking, attorneys are informed that CSRs have a 24-hour grace period. The system suggests setting the deadline one day before the attorney's true need-by date.

---

## Platform check-ins during active jobs

The system pings the CSR during the job to keep delivery on track:

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Midpoint of delivery window | "How's it going? Is this job on track?" (Yes/No) |
| CSR answers "No" | System asks: "Would you like to release this job for reassignment?" |
| CSR declines reassignment | One more check-in closer to deadline |
| Second check-in: still not on track | Job automatically reassigned |
| **Rush/Expedited jobs:** CSR says not on track | Job automatically reassigned immediately (no second chance) |

## Strike system

| Event | Consequence |
|-------|------------|
| 1st late delivery or quality complaint | Warning + notation on internal record |
| 2nd–4th offense | Warning + notation; patterns surfaced to admin |
| **5th offense** | Account suspended |
| Suspension appeal | CSR can appeal to Dewey Sholes admin; reviewed within 5 business days |

**Quality complaints** include: transcript delivered with significant errors, wrong case delivered, missing certification page, or unprofessional conduct reported by attorney.

**What does NOT count as a strike:** Releasing a job through the proper release flow (see release flow policy), court-appointed CSRs pulled away by the court, or genuine emergencies documented at time of release.

---

## CSR release flow

When a CSR needs to give up an accepted job:

1. CSR initiates release from their dashboard
2. CSR selects reason: court appointment, illness, scheduling conflict, personal emergency, other
3. CSR indicates when they knew about the conflict (just now, within 24 hours, more than 24 hours ago)
4. Job re-enters the matching algorithm:
   - If original deadline is 5+ days out → normal matching cascade
   - If deadline is < 5 days → rush/expedited flow
5. Release reason and timing logged for admin review

**Release frequency tracking:** Releases don't directly affect ratings, but admin monitors release frequency. A CSR who releases 30%+ of accepted jobs triggers an admin review conversation — not automated punishment.

---

## Court-appointed CSR handling

CSRs who also work as court employees get special treatment:

- **Profile badge:** "Court-appointed" badge visible to attorneys, so they know this CSR may be pulled away by court obligations
- **Release flow:** Court appointment is a valid release reason with no penalty
- **Transparency:** Attorney sees at booking: "This reporter also serves as a court-appointed CSR and may occasionally need to release a job due to court obligations."
