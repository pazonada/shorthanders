# Transcript correction policy

**Owner:** Aud & Rosie
**Last updated:** 2026-03-26
**Status:** Draft — confirmed in Rosie call 3/26

---

## Context

Per Rosie (22 years in the industry): "I have literally never heard in over 20 years of a court reporter's transcription error leading to anything that anyone cares about, other than tiny typos that are insignificant."

CSRs guarantee 100% accuracy under their professional certification (signed under penalty of perjury), but in practice, minor errors are common and rarely consequential. Statutory procedures already exist for challenging transcripts in criminal proceedings where material facts are misrepresented.

---

## Platform position

Dewey Sholes does **not** adjudicate transcript accuracy. The reporter's professional certification is the accuracy guarantee. Our role is limited to:

1. Facilitating delivery of the transcript
2. Providing a mechanism for attorneys to flag issues
3. Connecting the attorney and reporter to resolve disputes

---

## Correction flow

1. Attorney flags an issue through the platform ("Request correction" button in transcript review)
2. Reporter is notified and reviews the flagged sections
3. Reporter uploads corrected transcript (using the withdraw/re-upload flow)
4. Correction is logged in the job history

**No additional cost to the attorney for corrections.** The reporter's statutory rate covers delivering an accurate transcript.

---

## Time limit for correction requests

- Corrections can be requested within **90 days** of delivery acceptance (aligns with audio retention period)
- After 90 days, the attorney must contact the reporter directly (outside the platform)

---

## Deferred to pilot phase

Per Rosie: "This is not a big thing to worry about right now. Let's return to this scenario at the pilot phase."

During pilot, handle correction requests case-by-case. If patterns emerge (e.g., one reporter consistently producing error-heavy transcripts), address through the strike/deplatforming system.
