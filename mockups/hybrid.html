Three ways to get your record.

Dewey Sholes supports you whether the public hearing is upcoming, has already happened, or someone else already ordered the transcript. Depositions and private proceedings are not supported at this time.

Use case A

Live attendance

Need a reporter at an upcoming hearing? We'll match you with a licensed CSR who can attend in person or remotely and deliver your transcript on the turnaround you choose.

  • In-person or remote attendance
  • Real-time or same-day options
  • Reporter confirmed before your hearing
  • Cost-splitting across parties built in
33% below agency rates + statutory transcript rate
Use case B

Post-hearing transcription

Already have the court audio? Upload it and we'll match you with a certified reporter who'll transcribe, certify, and deliver, with your case docs pre-loaded for accuracy.

  • Upload MP3, WAV, or court-format audio
  • Track job progress in real time
  • Message your CSR directly through the platform
  • Supporting docs reduce back-and-forth
Use case C

Find the reporter for an existing case

Need a copy of a transcript already created in your case? Search by case number to find the original CSR and request a copy directly from them. Per California law, all parties will be notified before the CSR releases the copy.

Where does your money go?

Most agencies keep the majority of what you pay. We don't.

Traditional agency
Reporter sees 15–50%
Agencies mark up the statutory rate by 50–85%. The reporter who did the work may see as little as 16 cents of every dollar you paid.
Dewey Sholes
Reporter keeps 100%
You pay the statutory rate plus a flat 12% platform fee. The reporter receives the full statutory rate – no middleman cut, no markup.
The 12% platform fee is paid by the attorney. Reporters pay a flat annual listing fee only – no per-job deductions.
