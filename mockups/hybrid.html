How it works

Three steps, and you're on the record.

The whole flow is a fifteen-minute walk-through. No sales demo, no onboarding call.

01.

Open a case file.

Tell us the court, department, date, and approximate length. Got a docket number? Even better. We auto-fill what we can from public court calendars.

~ 90 seconds · incl. captcha
02.

Match a certified reporter.

We surface CSRs available for your date and location – with active license numbers, sample work, and reporter-set rates. You choose; we confirm.

3 reporters reviewed · under 5 minutes
03.

Receive your certified transcript.

Delivered as PDF and ASCII e-tran. Cited line numbers, certified signature page, ready to file. Standard, expedited, or daily turnaround.

What we cover

Two ways to get on the record.

Whether you need a reporter in the room or just a clean transcript of public-record audio, Dewey Sholes handles both.

Option A · Live

Live attendance.

A certified shorthand reporter appears at your hearing, in person or via authorized remote setup, and produces the official transcript.

  • Reporter assigned, confirmed, and credentialed to the court
  • Same-day rough transcript available on request
  • Daily, expedited, and standard turnaround options
  • Reporter rates set by statute, not by an agency
Schedule live attendance →
Option B · Audio

Audio transcription.

You already have the public-record audio – a courtroom mic, a clerk's recording, a public hearing. We transcribe it, certify it, and deliver it.

  • Certified by a California CSR upon completion
  • 24-hour rush available for hearings under 4 hours
  • ASCII, PDF, and e-tran delivery formats
  • Citation-ready line numbering and page-and-line index

For reporters

Keep your money.

Most agencies pay reporters 40–60% of the statutory rate, then bill the firm the full schedule. We don't do that. Reporters set their own rates, and what the attorney pays is what the reporter earns.

01.

Set your own rate.

Within statutory limits, you decide what to charge. No agency margin, no take-it-or-leave-it desk rate. The dashboard is yours.

02.

Choose your own work.

See the case, the court, the department, and the date before you accept. No surprise depo hours on a hearing booking. No mystery files.

03.

Get paid on time.

Net-14 from upload. No chasing AP. No follow-up emails to the firm. We escrow on order and release on delivery.
