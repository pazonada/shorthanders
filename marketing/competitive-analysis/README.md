## Directory/marketplace segment sweep (2026-08-04)

**Why this section exists:** this folder had historically over-indexed on funded AI-transcription and full-service agencies (see Adjacent products + Direct-competitors-as-agency below) and under-covered DS's actual peer set – **directories and marketplaces that let firms find and directly hire reporters**, without an agency in the middle. This sweep is a deliberate pass at that specific segment, separated explicitly from agencies.

**Segment read: not sparse.** There are at least five real, live directory/marketplace products beyond DS serving some version of "attorney finds and books a reporter directly." This is a genuinely active segment, not an empty lane DS has to itself – the differentiation has to be on execution (messaging, payments, rate transparency, complete CA/IL supply from public data) rather than on being the only player with the idea. **eCourt Reporters and CourtReporters.com now have full profiles** (see the Direct competitors table above); the rest below are still one-line reads pending a closer look.

### Directory/marketplace (DS's lane – attorney finds/books a reporter directly, no agency intermediary)

| Name | One-line read | Full-profile priority |
|---|---|---|
| **AppearMe** (appearme.com) | Real-time, job-post-and-accept marketplace ("post your case, get offers, connect in seconds") – closer to a job board than a browsable directory. Two-sided, verified signup, "fully automated" through payment (mechanics not confirmed). Explicitly mentions **Illinois** in its coverage – directly relevant to DS's next expansion state. Claims 10,000+ professionals on the network (unverified) and offers "first appearance free." | **High** – job-board mechanic + explicit Illinois presence make this the most relevant single remaining find for DS's IL expansion planning |
| **CourtScribes** (courtscribes.com) | Positions as a tech-forward agency/marketplace hybrid – "Hire a Court Reporter Today" self-serve booking language, but pricing shown ($5/page, day rates) reads like standard agency pricing rather than a reporter-set-your-own-rate marketplace. Ambiguous whether reporters have independent accounts or whether this is CourtScribes-as-agency with an online booking front end. | Medium – worth a closer look specifically to resolve the agency-vs-marketplace ambiguity |
| **NNRC** (National Network Reporting Company, nnrc.com) | "World's most extensive directory of court reporting firms" – but it's a **directory of firms/agencies**, not individual reporters, and not attorney-to-reporter direct booking. Closer to a B2B agency-referral network than a DS-style marketplace. | Low – different unit of listing (firms, not individual reporters); monitor only |

### Trade-association / nonprofit directories (reference, not commercial competitors)

| Name | One-line read |
|---|---|
| **NCRA PROLink** (ncra.org) | The National Court Reporters Association's own member directory – opt-in for NCRA members, searchable by locale/certification/services. Free, membership-gated, not a booking or payment platform. Useful as a reference point for "what a trade-association directory looks like," not a competitive threat. |
| **Cal-CCRA "Find a Reporter"** (cal-ccra.org) | California Court Reporters Association's member-search page. Same pattern as NCRA PROLink – member directory, not a marketplace. |
| **StenoSearch** (stenosearch.com) | Legacy CA court-reporter directory – copyright year 2016, listing dates as old as 2010-2014, early-2000s web design. Commercially operated (ad space, paid listings) but clearly stale/unmaintained. Low relevance; noted for completeness only. |

### Explicitly NOT this segment (checked and ruled out)

- **CoCounsel.ai** – despite ranking for "direct-to-attorney court reporting" searches, this is an **AI real-time-transcription tool that replaces the human reporter with speech-to-text**, not a directory/marketplace for finding a human reporter. Belongs conceptually with the existing Adjacent-products AI-transcription set (Verbit, Colibri, VoiceScript), not this sweep. No entry needed beyond this note unless it adds a human-reporter marketplace layer later.
- **Guru, ZipRecruiter** – generic freelance/job-posting platforms that happen to return court-reporter results; not court-reporting-specific products and not a meaningful competitive signal.
- **LawNext Directory** – a legal-tech review/comparison aggregator site (lists "Best Court Reporting Software"), not itself a marketplace or directory of reporters.

## Watching but not yet profiled

Sites or products that surfaced during research but don't yet warrant a full entry – either too thin to evaluate or clearly outside our competitive radius. (Directory/marketplace-segment finds from the 2026-08-04 sweep are tracked separately above, not duplicated here.)

- **RepAgencyWorks** (devel.repagencyworks.com) – court reporting agency back-office software; appears to be pre-launch or in dev (domain prefix `devel.`). Serves agencies, not individual CSRs or attorneys.
- **DepoDash** – remote deposition infrastructure; deposition-only scope, no court-hearing angle. Monitor if they add hearing-transcript functionality.
- **DepoDirect** – remote deposition platform targeting "virtual depositions" without reporters; AI-only model. Outside V1 scope but watch for expansion into transcript delivery.
- **Planet Depos / Veritext / Esquire** – large incumbent agencies with online booking portals. Not a tech marketplace; their model is what DS disrupts. Monitor for any self-serve marketplace moves.
- **MyTexasCSR** (mytexascsr.com) – a Texas job board for court reporting assignments, run jointly
  by the two Texas trade associations (TCRA + TEXDRA). Surfaced 2026-07-29 while researching
  partner orgs for the Texas expansion (see `mytexascsr.md` stub). Looks like a staffing job board,
  not a directory + messaging + payments product like DS – but it has built-in distribution to
  every Texas freelance reporter, which DS won't have on day one there. Needs a real profile before
  Texas launch, not just this stub.
