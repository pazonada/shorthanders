## How to use this folder

Before drafting positioning copy, updating a tagline, choosing a new channel, or making a pricing decision, the marketing-strategist agent should:

1. Re-read this README to orient to the current competitive map.
2. Cross-check specific claims against the relevant detailed entry (e.g., before claiming "no one else serves CA court hearings" – verify against the direct-competitor entries).
3. Update the "Last reviewed" date in any entry you re-verified during a task.
4. If a new competitor surfaces during a task, create a stub entry (can be a partial fill of `_template.md`) and add it to the appropriate tier below before finishing the task.

For the founding-member value proposition, see [`governance-and-process/founding_member_program.md`](../../governance-and-process/founding_member_program.md). For channel and positioning strategy, see [`marketing/marketing_strategy_master.md`](../marketing_strategy_master.md).

## Watching but not yet profiled

Sites or products that surfaced during research but don't yet warrant a full entry – either too thin to evaluate or clearly outside our competitive radius.

- **RepAgencyWorks** (devel.repagencyworks.com) – court reporting agency back-office software; appears to be pre-launch or in dev (domain prefix `devel.`). Serves agencies, not individual CSRs or attorneys.
- **DepoDash** – remote deposition infrastructure; deposition-only scope, no court-hearing angle. Monitor if they add hearing-transcript functionality.
- **DepoDirect** – remote deposition platform targeting "virtual depositions" without reporters; AI-only model. Outside V1 scope but watch for expansion into transcript delivery.
- **Planet Depos / Veritext / Esquire** – large incumbent agencies with online booking portals. Not a tech marketplace; their model is what DS disrupts. Monitor for any self-serve marketplace moves.
- **MyTexasCSR** (mytexascsr.com) – a Texas job board for court reporting assignments, run jointly
  by the two Texas trade associations (TCRA + TEXDRA). Surfaced 2026-07-29 while researching
  partner orgs for the Texas expansion (see `mytexascsr.md` stub). Looks like a staffing job board,
  not a directory + messaging + payments product like DS – but it has built-in distribution to
  every Texas freelance reporter, which DS won't have on day one there. Needs a real profile before
  Texas launch, not just this stub.
