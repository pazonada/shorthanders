## One-line pitch

> "Where court reporters connect with legal professionals" – a job marketplace for posting and
> applying to court reporting assignments (depositions, hearings, meetings) across Texas.

> Not captured verbatim this pass – see site directly if this entry gets promoted to a full profile.

## Target customer

Both sides: legal professionals post assignments; Texas court reporters browse and apply
(including for substitute/fill-in work). No membership in either trade association required to
join, post, or apply.

---

## Pricing model

- **Model type:** not researched this pass (appears to be a free job board run as a member service,
  not a paid product – unconfirmed)

---

## Geographic scope

Texas only.

---

## Service scope

Depositions, hearings, and "meetings" – broader than DS's current CA scope note: this looks like
job-posting/booking (who's available for a specific assignment), not a full directory + messaging +
invoicing product like DS. Needs a closer look before we expand to Texas.

---

## CSR-supply model

Any Texas court reporter can create a profile; not gated by trade-association membership.

---

## Visible feature set

Post a job listing; browse/apply to listings; create a profile "within minutes." Site explicitly
states it does not screen positions or parties involved.

## Build maturity signals

Not assessed this pass – found via search while researching Texas trade associations, not a full

## Strengths vs. DS

- **Owned by the two Texas trade associations (TCRA + TEXDRA) jointly** – built-in distribution to
  the entire Texas freelance-reporter population without needing a separate courtship. That's a
  real structural advantage DS doesn't have anywhere yet.

---

## Weaknesses vs. DS

- Looks like a job board (post/apply to individual assignments), not a directory + profile +
  messaging + get-paid product. If that's accurate, it doesn't compete with DS's core value prop
  (find and directly message/pay a specific reporter you choose) so much as with ad-hoc staffing.
- No visible money-model transparency pitch (DS's core differentiator vs. agencies).

---

## Overlap with our positioning

Unclear yet – possibly none, if it's purely a staffing job board rather than a reporter-selection
and pricing-transparency product. Needs a real look before the Texas expansion (see
`mvp/marketing/growth_strategy.md` – state expansion order, Texas first).
